## Readiness, interruption and resolved repeats

Offer acknowledgements optionally in their valid safe presentation context, clear
of gameplay transitions. Duo acting requires both protagonists able to speak;
otherwise use the current cue. That readiness requirement gates only acting, not
the real challenge, descent, free service or retreat. No dialogue heals, revives,
refills actions, consumes resources, starts a fight or performs a descent.

Completed acknowledgements never replay on re-entry or cold load. Skipped,
interrupted or not-ready ones stay optional on request in a legal current context,
never auto-start on return and block no outcome or service. Re-read real state
before any later request. After victory, suppress stale unresolved inspection
wording; after actual completion, suppress pending pre-descent victory wording
and use the completion cue. Same-state resolved inspections use concise cues
without repeating character reactions, rewards or AI interruptions. Once-only
presentation bookkeeping awaits F1-S01 and remains independent of all outcomes.

Interruption before genuine victory leaves it unearned; interruption of its
acknowledgement afterward cannot undo it or demand another challenge. Interruption
before the real descent leaves floor completion unearned; interruption of text
after actual completion cannot revoke it or rerun descent/rewards. This states
narrative behavior only; combat and completion transaction/persistence design
remain separate production work.

With depleted uses or either protagonist incapacitated on a safe field approach
or post-victory/pre-descent return, preserve ordinary retreat to the Causeway
refuge and its established free recovery/manual Save outside combat. No quest,
payment, consumable, optional preparation or acknowledgement is required. Use
the [Causeway recovery/return guidance](causeway-refuge-scene-brief.md#final-approach-and-return-f1-d8-routes)
for existing onward/retreat connections; this brief adds no new service or path.
